VPN Student Russia — VPN & foreground service demo

Screen recording of the release build on Android 15:

  1. The user taps Connect. The app starts its VpnService as a user-initiated foreground service.
  2. The status changes to Connected, with live connection time and traffic. The VPN key icon appears in the status bar.
  3. The persistent notification shows the server name, the status and a Disconnect button.
  4. The user taps Disconnect in the notification. The tunnel and the foreground service stop and the notification is removed.
