FAQ

Does a VPN make me anonymous?
No. It encrypts traffic between your device and our server and hides your IP address from the websites you visit, but it does not make you anonymous.

Why do I see ads?
The app is free; ads pay for the servers. You can change your ad privacy choices in the app under Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

How do I delete my data?
We do not keep an account or activity logs. Uninstall the app to remove local data, and see the privacy policy for more.
