VPN Student Russia — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VPN Student Russia ("we", "us") handles information when you use the VPN Student Russia mobile app for Android and iOS (the "App") and our VPN servers (together, the "Service").

Summary

  • No account. You do not sign up, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the App.
  • No activity logs. We do not log the websites you visit, the content of your traffic, your DNS queries, or the apps you use through the VPN.
  • Connection data is processed only to run the VPN. While you are connected, our VPN servers necessarily process your device's IP address and connection time in memory in order to route your traffic. We do not store this in logs linked to you.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, subject to your consent where required.
  • We do not sell your data for money. Ads may involve "sharing" as defined by some US state laws; you can opt out in the App.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store:

  • your name, email, phone number, postal address or payment information (the App is free and has no account or in-app purchases);
  • your browsing history, visited domains or URLs, traffic content, or DNS queries;
  • the originating IP address associated with the websites you visit through the VPN;
  • your contacts, photos, files, precise (GPS) location, or microphone/camera data.

2. Information processed to provide the VPN

Connection data. To create the encrypted tunnel and forward your traffic, our VPN servers process, in memory and only for the duration of the connection: your device's public IP address, the time of the connection, and the amount of data transferred. Our servers are configured not to write access logs. We may keep aggregate, non-identifying statistics per server (for example total bandwidth or number of simultaneous connections) to plan capacity and prevent abuse. These statistics cannot be linked to you.

Server list. The list of servers is built into the App.

Connection check. Right after you connect, the App sends one small request (GET /generate_204) through the VPN to a public connectivity-check endpoint (Cloudflare cp.cloudflare.com, or as a fallback Google connectivitycheck.gstatic.com / Apple captive.apple.com) to confirm that the server is reachable. The request contains no personal data and comes from the VPN server's IP address, not yours.

Servers you add yourself. The App lets you add your own server from a vless:// link. Traffic sent through such a server is handled by whoever operates it, not by us, under their own privacy practices. Links you add are stored only on your device.

Server locations. Our VPN servers are located in Sweden. When connected, websites you visit see the IP address of the VPN server instead of yours.

Data stored on your device. The App stores on your device: your settings (selected server, language), the VPN configuration required by the operating system, your ad-consent choices, and timestamps used to limit how often ads are shown. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). The App shows full-screen ads when it is opened and after a VPN connection is established.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may collect and process:

  • device identifiers — Android Advertising ID / App Set ID, or on iOS the IDFA (only if you allow tracking) and IDFV;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • usage and ad data — ad impressions, clicks, app launches;
  • diagnostic data — crash and performance information of the ad SDK.

Google uses this data to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. For details see "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we ask for your consent through Google's UMP consent form (IAB TCF v2.2) before personalised ads are shown. You can accept, refuse, or manage options. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • iOS: the App asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, the IDFA is not available to the App or to Google.
  • Any time: open Settings → Privacy choices in the App (shown where a consent choice applies) to review or change your choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Ads never prevent the VPN from working.

4. Permissions

  • VPN (Android "VPN connection request", iOS "Add VPN Configurations"): required to create the encrypted tunnel. Requested only when you tap Connect.
  • Notifications (Android 13+): to show the persistent VPN status notification with a Disconnect button.
  • Tracking (iOS, optional): see Section 3.

5. How we use and share information

We use the information described above only to provide, secure and improve the Service and to show ads. We do not sell personal information for money. We share information only:

  • with Google for advertising as described in Section 3;
  • with hosting/infrastructure providers that run our servers, under contracts that require them to protect the data;
  • if required by law. Because we do not keep activity logs, we have no browsing history or traffic content to provide in response to a legal request.

7. Security

Traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted (VLESS protocol with REALITY/TLS 1.3). We restrict administrative access to our servers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.
